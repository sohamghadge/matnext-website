A-07Data Ingestion
Data Quality Agent
01-Origin02-Processing03-Manufacture04-Assembly
Three-layer validation gatekeeper for all steel flow data. Completeness check: every scrap bundle lot must have weight, grade classification, source facility ID, and collection date before a custody record is created. Consistency check: the mass balance across a Heat must close — the sum of all scrap and virgin iron inputs declared must equal the tap weight plus slag and fume losses within a defined tolerance (typically ±2% for EAF steelmaking). Any balance gap triggers immediate investigation before the Heat number is confirmed. Plausibility check: validates that the declared recycled content percentage is achievable given the actual input materials — if Shell Steel Bales Scrap and Other Steel Scrap account for 75% of inputs by weight, a claim of 90% recycled content is flagged as implausible unless additional verified PCR sources are present. Learns quality patterns per scrap supplier.
