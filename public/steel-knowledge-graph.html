What Makes the Steel Knowledge Graph Distinct
The Heat number as primary traceability unit, tramp element accumulation as a cross-generational data structure, and 5-tier manufacturing genealogy
Steel-Specific Architecture
The Heat Number: Steel's Primary Traceability Node
In the PP plastic flow, the pellet lot is the primary traceability unit. In the steel circular chain, the Heat number takes this role — a unique identifier assigned to each EAF melt campaign (typically 100–180 tonnes). A single Heat merges inputs from multiple scrap bundles, PCR/PIR lots and virgin iron sources, making the Heat node the most data-dense entity in the graph. Every downstream product — billets, bars, forgings, machined parts — inherits its recycled content, chemistry declaration and carbon footprint from the Heat node that created the liquid steel. This parent-child inheritance is the core of steel traceability: ELV scrap bundles → :Heat → :Billet → :Bar → :ForgedBlank → :MachinedPart → :Vehicle.
Cross-Generational Data Structure
Tramp Element Accumulation — A Unique Steel Graph Feature
Steel's circular economy has a fundamental challenge that plastics do not: tramp elements (copper Cu, tin Sn, lead Pb, antimony Sb) cannot be removed during EAF steelmaking and accumulate with every recycling generation. The MANJU steel graph stores the full tramp element history on every :Heat node and propagates it through all downstream :Part nodes. Agent A-33 (Anomaly Detection) runs cross-Heat Cu accumulation trend analysis — querying the graph for Cu concentration trajectories across successive Heats from the same scrap supplier. This cross-generational intelligence is impossible in a relational database but trivial in a property graph: MATCH path = (:ScrapSupplier)-[:SUPPLIED_TO*1..20]->(:Heat) RETURN nodes(path), [n IN nodes(path) | n.cu_pct].
Five-Tier Manufacturing Genealogy
Deeper Traversal Than Plastics — 5 Manufacturing Tiers
The steel chain has a deeper manufacturing hierarchy than the PP plastic chain. Where PP goes Recycler → Moulder → OEM (3 nodes), steel goes Recycler → Tier 3 Forging → Tier 2 Machining → Tier 1 Assembly → OEM (5 nodes). Each tier adds a new layer of part transformation, dimensional inspection data and energy consumption records. The MANJU graph handles this with variable-depth [:TRANSFORMED_INTO*1..5] traversal patterns — allowing a single query to follow the material from any point in the chain to any other point regardless of depth. A gear serial can be traced to its source ELV VINs through 7 graph hops in under 50ms.

Seven Semantic Layers of the Steel Graph
The steel graph has the same 7-layer architecture as the PP graph but with steel-specific node types and data — most notably the Chemistry & Tramp Element layer which has no equivalent in plastics
Layer 1
Physical Steel Flow Layer
The physical ground truth of every tonne of steel in the system — from scrap bundle to EAF Heat, billet, bar, forging and machined part. The Heat node is the transformation pivot: inputs merge into liquid steel; outputs are solid billets with a new identity but inherited genealogy.
:ScrapBundle:Heat:Billet:BarLot:ForgedBlank:MachinedPart:AssemblyUnit:MELTED_INTO edges:CAST_INTO edges:ROLLED_INTO edgestap_weight_tonnes
Layer 2
Provenance & Custody Layer
Legal and physical chain of custody for steel scrap and products across the 5-tier manufacturing chain. Mass balance validation at every transfer — from scrap delivery to the EAF gate, through each tier handoff to the OEM assembly line. ATF and IATF 16949 certifications checked at each processing event.
:CustodyTransfer nodes:Organisation nodes:OWNED_BY edges:DELIVERED_TO edgesmass_balance_tonnesatf_validated: booliatf_valid: boolradiation_screened: bool
Layer 3
Chemistry & Tramp Element Layer
Unique to steel — no equivalent in the PP graph. Every Heat node has a linked :ChemistryRecord storing the full spectrometry analysis. The tramp element sub-layer (Cu, Sn, Pb, Sb) tracks concentrations across successive Heats from each scrap supplier, enabling the cross-generational accumulation analysis that is critical for automotive steel quality assurance.
:ChemistryRecord nodes:TrampElementLog nodes:HAS_CHEMISTRY edges:EXCEEDS_LIMIT edgescu_pct, sn_pct, pb_pct, sb_pctcarbon_equivalent_ceautomotive_limit_met: bool
Layer 4
Compliance & Regulatory Layer
Links every scrap bundle, Heat and part to the regulations it must comply with. When a regulation changes, traversal from the :Regulation node finds every affected :Heat or :Part instantly. The CBAM regulation is uniquely present here — linking imported pig iron and DRI inputs to their declared embedded carbon costs.
:Regulation nodes:ComplianceRecord nodes:COMPLIES_WITH edges:VIOLATES edgescbam_certificate_reffiling_iddeadline_date
Layer 5
Carbon Footprint Layer
Every Heat, bar lot and steel part carries a linked :CarbonRecord documenting its ISO 14067 PCF. For steel, EAF electricity consumption is the dominant variable (~70% of total Heat PCF). The layer stores the critical EAF vs BOF comparison: ~0.52 kg CO₂e/kg (EAF, 88% recycled) vs ~2.05 kg CO₂e/kg (BOF, virgin) — the 75% carbon reduction claim evidenced per Heat.
:CarbonRecord nodes:HAS_CARBON_FOOTPRINT edgeseaf_energy_kgco2eforging_energy_kgco2eheat_treatment_kgco2eeaf_vs_bof_reduction_pct
Layer 6
Digital Passport Layer
The official output layer — every :DigitalProductPassport node aggregates data from the five layers below it and always carries a reference to the originating Heat number. The Mill Test Certificate (MTC) is the steel-specific document type that serves as the DPP for bar lots delivered to Tier 3 forge shops.
:DigitalProductPassport nodes:HAS_PASSPORT edges:SUPERSEDES edgesheat_number_ref (always)mtc_reference (for bar lots)version: intppap_ref (Tier 1 → OEM)
Layer 7
Agent Audit Layer
Provenance-of-the-provenance for steel. Every node created by an agent — including the metallurgist's Heat approval gate record — is linked to the :AgentAction that created it. The metallurgist's human approval of Heat chemistry is permanently embedded in the graph with approver identity, timestamp and the specific chemistry values approved.
:AgentAction nodes:CREATED_BY edges:METALLURGIST_APPROVED edgesmetallurgist_idchemistry_approved_values{}sha256_hash
Cross-Layer
5-Tier Supply Network Layer
Maps the 5-tier supply network — ELV Dismantlers → EAF Recycler → Tier 3 Forge → Tier 2 Machining → Tier 1 Assembly → OEM — including the shared Tier 2 supplier risk pattern where the same machining facility serves multiple Tier 1 plants, creating a hidden OEM concentration risk.

Steel-Specific Graph Queries — Questions Only Possible With Connected Data
Six Cypher queries that demonstrate the unique intelligence the steel graph enables — from tramp element trend detection to 7-hop ELV traceability
Traceability · 7-Hop Reverse
"Which ELV vehicles contributed steel to gear serial #GR-88421 and what was the Cu level in the Heat?"
MATCH (v:Vehicle)-[:ASSEMBLED_WITH]->(p:SteelPart {serial:'GR-88421'})
-[:MACHINED_FROM]->(f:ForgedBlank)
-[:FORGED_FROM]->(b:BarLot)
-[:ROLLED_FROM]->(bil:Billet)
-[:CAST_FROM]->(h:Heat)
-[:MELTED_INTO {source:'scrap'}]->(s:ScrapBundle)
-[:ORIGINATES_FROM_ELV]->(elv:Vehicle)
MATCH (h)-[:HAS_CHEMISTRY]->(chem:ChemistryRecord)
RETURN elv.vin, s.grade, h.heat_number,
chem.cu_pct, s.weight_tonnes
Returns: All source ELV VINs and scrap grades contributing steel to gear GR-88421, with the Cu concentration in the Heat. 7 graph hops in <50ms. Would require 14+ table joins in SQL.
Anomaly · Tramp Accumulation
"Is the Cu level in Heats from scrap supplier ORG-042 trending upward and when will it breach the 0.08% automotive limit?"
MATCH (org:Organisation {org_id:'ORG-042'})
-[:SUPPLIED]->(s:ScrapBundle)
-[:MELTED_INTO]->(h:Heat)
-[:HAS_CHEMISTRY]->(chem:ChemistryRecord)
WHERE h.production_date > datetime() - duration('P6M')
WITH org, h.production_date as dt,
chem.cu_pct as cu ORDER BY dt
WITH collect(cu) as cu_series,
collect(dt) as dates
RETURN cu_series, dates,
(cu_series[-1]-cu_series[0])/size(cu_series) as trend_pct_per_heat
Returns: Cu time-series per supplier with trend slope. If trend_pct_per_heat = +0.003%, Agent A-33 projects breach in N Heats and alerts procurement — before the automotive limit is actually crossed.
Carbon · PCF Full Stack
"What is the complete ISO 14067 cradle-to-gate carbon footprint of wheel hub WH-2291 on Vehicle VIN DE-X99841?"
MATCH (v:Vehicle {vin:'DE-X99841'})
-[:ASSEMBLED_WITH {position:'FrontHub'}]->(p:SteelPart)
-[:HAS_CARBON_FOOTPRINT]->(ppcf:CarbonRecord)
MATCH (p)-[:MACHINED_FROM*1..3]->(b:BarLot)
-[:HAS_CARBON_FOOTPRINT]->(bpcf:CarbonRecord)
MATCH (b)-[:ROLLED_FROM]->(bil:Billet)
-[:CAST_FROM]->(h:Heat)
-[:HAS_CARBON_FOOTPRINT]->(hpcf:CarbonRecord)
RETURN p.part_serial, ppcf.pcf_kgco2e,
hpcf.eaf_energy_kgco2e,
hpcf.eaf_vs_bof_reduction_pct
Returns: Part-level PCF with upstream breakdown — EAF energy (dominant), forging, machining, heat treatment. Shows ~75% carbon reduction vs BOF virgin steel. ISO 14067 compliant, audit-ready for CSRD.
Recycled Content · Heat Certification
"What is the verified recycled content of Heat STEEL-2024-088 and is there any double-counting risk across concurrent Heats?"
MATCH (h:Heat {heat_number:'STEEL-2024-088'})
-[m:MELTED_INTO]->(s:ScrapBundle)
WHERE s.grade IN ['ShellSteelBales','OtherSteelScrap','PCR','PIR']
WITH h, sum(m.fraction_pct) as recycled_pct,
collect({bundle:s.bundle_id, lock:m.anti_double_count_lock_id}) as locks
MATCH (h2:Heat)-[m2:MELTED_INTO]->(s)
WHERE h2.heat_number <> h.heat_number
AND h2.production_date > datetime()-duration('P7D')
RETURN recycled_pct,
[l IN locks WHERE l.lock IS NULL] as unlocked_risk
Returns: 88% verified recycled content with lock IDs on all claimed scrap fractions. Checks prior 7 days for concurrent Heats claiming the same scrap — anti-double-counting enforced by Agent A-15.
Supply Risk · IATF Expiry
"Which Tier 3 forge shops in the OEM's steel supply chain have IATF 16949 certifications expiring in the next 6 months?"
MATCH (oem:Organisation {role:'OEM'})
<-[:SUPPLIES_TO*1..4]-(t3:Organisation {role:'Tier3_Forge'})
-[:IATF_CERTIFIED_BY]->(cert:Certificate {cert_type:'IATF16949'})
WHERE cert.valid_to < datetime() + duration('P6M')
AND cert.valid_to > datetime()
RETURN t3.name, t3.org_id, cert.valid_to,
duration.between(datetime(),cert.valid_to).months as months_remaining
ORDER BY cert.valid_to ASC
Returns: Ranked IATF 16949 expiry risk for all Tier 3 forge shops in the OEM's steel chain. Agent A-23 uses this query monthly — lapse means OEM must halt purchasing from that forge shop immediately.
Chemistry · Forge Quality Prediction
"Which bar lots currently in the Tier 3 forge shop queue have chemistry or CE values that predict elevated surface crack risk in gear production?"
MATCH (b:BarLot {status:'InForgeQueue'})
-[:ROLLED_FROM]->(bil:Billet)
-[:CAST_FROM]->(h:Heat)
-[:HAS_CHEMISTRY]->(chem:ChemistryRecord)
WHERE chem.carbon_equivalent_ce > 0.52
OR chem.cu_pct > 0.075
WITH b, chem,
CASE WHEN chem.carbon_equivalent_ce > 0.52
AND chem.cu_pct > 0.075
THEN 'HIGH' ELSE 'MEDIUM' END as risk_level
RETURN b.bar_lot_id, chem.carbon_equivalent_ce,
chem.cu_pct, risk_level
ORDER BY risk_level DESC
Returns: Ranked forge queue risk list. Agent A-34 uses this pre-production to alert the forge shop — HIGH risk lots (CE >0.52 AND Cu >0.075%) have 3.2× higher surface crack rate if forging temperature drops below 1150°C.

Steel Graph Storage Architecture — Four Complementary Stores
The same 4-store architecture as the PP graph — but with steel-specific data volumes, retention requirements and real-time needs driven by EAF process control
🌐
Property Graph Database
Neo4j Enterprise · Primary Steel Query Engine
Primary operational store for all steel traversal queries — Heat genealogy tracing, tramp element accumulation analysis, IATF 16949 certification status checks and passport assembly. The Heat-to-gear 7-hop traversal executes in under 50ms via indexed heat_number properties and optimised relationship indexes on :MELTED_INTO and :CAST_INTO.
Indexes: heat_number (primary key), bar_lot_id, part_serial, vin — all B-tree indexed
Key index: Composite (cu_pct, production_date) on :ChemistryRecord — enables tramp trend queries
Replication: Multi-region causal cluster — EU-West primary, EU-Central replica
Access: Role-based Cypher grants per agent cluster — A-13 owns Heat genealogy writes exclusively
Special: :TrampElementLog nodes are indexed by (supplier_id, production_date) for trend queries
📊
Analytical Data Warehouse
Snowflake · Steel Analytics & CSRD
Handles bulk aggregations not suited to the graph — per-Heat carbon intensity trends across thousands of Heats, recycled content percentages by steel grade over time, CSRD disclosure calculations, and the EU CBAM embedded carbon calculations for imported iron inputs. Syncs from Neo4j every 15 minutes via Change Data Capture.
Key tables: fact_heat (per-Heat recycled%, PCF, chemistry), fact_part_pcf, dim_organisation, dim_regulation
Used by: A-19 (CSRD), A-21 (Carbon), A-32 (ESG), A-34 (Predictive Analytics — chemistry ML models)
CBAM table: imported_iron_embedded_carbon — carbon intensity per supplier per lot for CBAM filing
Retention: Full historical with time-travel — Heat records retained 10+ years for warranty traceability
⚡
Event Stream
Apache Kafka · Real-Time Steel Process Bus
